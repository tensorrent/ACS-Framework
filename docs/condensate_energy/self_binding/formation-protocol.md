# Formation from a charged packet in a uniform condensate

Registered after stationary and perturbation qualification, before these runs.
The existence of a constrained stationary solution is not a formation result.

Start chi=1 everywhere, chi_t=0. Set q=A exp[-r^2/(2 sigma^2)] with sigma=3,6,9
and q_t=i q; choose A once so Q=1000. This is an initially charged, spatially
localized coherent packet with omega_initial=1, not creation from empty vacuum
or capture of generic incident radiation. No condensate hole is preinserted.
Use b=2sqrt(3)/27, radius120,end80,dr0.1,dt0.005, all previous dynamical definitions.

Run these three widths, plus a dr0.05,dt0.0025 refinement of sigma6 and a matched
sigma6 control with chi fixed at1. That control starts with the same q, charge
and total energy; it removes condensate response. Record all outcomes without
tuning widths afterward. No claim of asymptotic settling is inferred from t80.

Retain energy drift <1e-3, charge drift <1e-8 and distant-zone energy <1e-8.
Require central-width local-energy curves to differ by <0.02 E0 on refinement.
The initial energies of matched coupled/frozen controls must agree to1e-10.
Report core condensate depletion, energy and signed charge within radius15,
and the final time window. Formation or dispersal is an outcome, not a pass gate.
