# Incoming-channel supplement

This supplement is specified after the primary experiment, before its own runs.
The first result rejects a universal retention-equals-transmission identity for
an initially occupied cavity. It does not reject an entry/capture interpretation.

Use the identical linear Hamiltonian, fixed cavity [0,4] and barrier [4,5].
Prepare an incoming complex Gaussian centered at x=30, envelope sigma=4, carrier
k=pi/4, traveling left: Phi=f(x), Phi_t=f'(x). Normalize actual discrete energy
to one. U0 in [0,4,9]; domain120, dx=.1, dt=.005, stop80. Independently refine
U0=4 at dx=.05,dt=.0025. Endpoints are remote during measurement.

Measure initial, peak and late energy in cavity+barrier, and signed integrated
outward flux. Retain energy conservation and flux gates <1e-8, tail <1e-8, and
refinement difference <.02. This broad packet contains a frequency distribution;
T(pi/4) is only a reference and is not fitted to the peak captured energy.

No absorbing capture channel, moving wall, nonlinear field interaction or
irreversible phase slip is added. A temporary increase in cavity energy tests
entry, not permanent matter formation. Preserve this distinction even if some
transmission and retained-energy values coincide numerically.
