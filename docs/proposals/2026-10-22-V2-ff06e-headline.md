## 3. Optional paper amendment (paste-ready, appended after the 24 September amendment)

```latex
\section*{Amendment: reproducing script for the headline table, 23 October 2026}
The table of \S\ref{sec:meas} is regenerated by
\path{code/hp_knife_suite/ff06e_headline.py} (seed $20260423$, $60$ surrogates,
permute-then-reinterpolate as in the 2026-07-02 remark; about $3$\,s for
$N=4000,\,2\times10^4,\,10^5$). The arithmetic, lag-1 and Wigner-shape witnesses are
those of \path{hp_vantage_points.py}. This paper gives no formula for the spacing-mean
and counting deviations; the script's readings are $|\overline{s}-1|$ and
$|N-\overline{N}(\gamma_N)|/N$. Both depend only on the window endpoints. The surrogate
preserves those to rounding, so their surrogate $\sigma$ is ${\sim}4\times10^{-15}$. Their
$z$ is the ratio of one rounding error to another, so it is withheld, not reported as
$1.0\sigma$. The counting reading gives $5.9\times10^{-6}$ at $N=10^5$ where the table prints
$1\times10^{-5}$, so it may not be the quantity the table used.

Re-run: the raw values reproduce to the printed digits (arithmetic $0.0922$ at
$2\times10^4$ and $0.0636$ at $10^5$; lag-1 $-0.357$), except Wigner shape ($0.02571$
against $0.0258$). The split reproduces. The $z$ values are single draws of a
$60$-surrogate $\sigma$ estimate:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}
\toprule
 & \multicolumn{2}{c}{$N=2\times10^4$} & \multicolumn{2}{c}{$N=10^5$} \\
witness & $K=60$ & $K=6000$ & $K=60$ & $K=6000$ \\
\midrule
arithmetic & $3611$ & $3259$ & $14\,748$ & $12\,765$ \\
lag-1      & $48.6$ & $51.5$ & $103.4$ & $113.4$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Across $100$ disjoint $60$-surrogate blocks, the printed $3030$ and $11\,497$ fall at the
$23$rd and $17$th percentiles, and the printed lag-1 $97$ falls at the $2$nd. The lag-1
amplification $51\to97$ (converged $51.5\to113.4$) is what a constant $r_1\approx-0.36$
gives against a permutation null with $\sigma\simeq N^{-1/2}$: $|r_1|\sqrt{N-1}=51.6$ and
$112.9$. It shows $r_1\neq0$ at fixed size. On its own it does not show structure being
resolved.
```
