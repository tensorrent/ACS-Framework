## Conjugation and the next question

The first positive mod-5 heights are approximately 6.18357819545 for χ and 4.13290370521 for its conjugate. For mod 7 they are approximately 5.19811619947 and 2.50937455293. Conjugation gives L(s, conjugate(χ)) = conjugate(L(conjugate(s), χ)); the height changes sign. Positive spectra differ.

The historical synthesis used a doubled positive χ contribution to approximate the two conjugate factors. Its justification is incorrect. The claimed full-Dedekind 5–6× ratio is withdrawn pending a recomputation using all distinct factors, including a certified mod-5 quadratic factor, with the same cutoff and weighting.
