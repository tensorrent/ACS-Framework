# Protected-charge audit: scope and tests before execution

2026-09-23. Continue the self-binding experiment by checking its missing ACS
charge. Preserve all prior experiment receipts and source manuscripts.

Fields and conventions: complex Phi(1,2,2), Delta(bar10,1,3), three left-handed
families psi_L(4,2,1), and three physical right-handed families psi_R(4,1,2).
For anomaly sums use left-handed conjugates psi_R^c. The historical printed
code sometimes labels Delta as 10; use the canonical manuscript's bar10,
which contracts two physical fundamental right-handed fermions. Do not mix
these conventions. Study continuous internal transformations commuting with
the gauge action, preserving the canonical kinetic metric. Do not claim to
classify nonlinear, spacetime, emergent, or topological symmetries.

Read the complete 17-quartic invariant basis from the frozen frontier archive,
including its holomorphic Delta quartic. Save only relevant source snapshots
with their nested archive provenance. The earlier completeness proof is
inherited; this audit tests charge and transformation properties independently.

1. Translate all 4 quadratic and 17 real quartic terms into Phi/Delta phase
   weights. A nonzero real or imaginary part of a holomorphic invariant breaks
   its continuous phase; choosing a real coefficient is not a U(1) symmetry.
2. Impose both Dirac Yukawas and the Majorana coupling. Compute exact nullspaces
   with and without the holomorphic Delta term. Include all 3-family phase
   variables, the aligned nonzero conjugate/direct Yukawa ratio, and explicit
   arbitrary Hermitian flavor-generator equations at rational invertible
   matrices. Record excluded/rank-deficient sectors as conditional, not selected.
3. Independently check the holomorphic Delta invariant using determinant
   polarization and Gaussian fourth moments. Verify nonzero full-field witness,
   gauge invariance, phase covariance and the rank-one-vacuum trap. Use 24 seeded
   generic field samples, 3 phase pairs each, and unitary gauge transforms;
   relative discrepancies must be <1e-10. Exact witnesses are separate from
   floating-point checks.
4. For a surviving classical charge compute mixed gauge anomaly coefficients
   with T(fund)=1/2 and three generations. Derive the trace constraints for
   arbitrary invertible Yukawas so a flavor-basis choice cannot hide an anomaly.
   Nonzero anomalies mean not an exact quantum global charge; they do not
   supply a decay rate or imply rapid destruction.
5. Enumerate residual family-uniform phase transformations and gauge-center
   actions. Include ordinary SU(2) instanton selection rules separately. Report
   the field-action quotient only, not a classification of line operators,
   gauge bundles, all discrete anomalies or generalized symmetries.
6. Follow the candidate through the neutral Delta vacuum using gauged B-L.
   Compute charges of quarks, leptons, and all symmetric Delta color components.
   Check whether the neutral mode can be the charged q of the previous model.
7. State what remains of the energy criterion once fermions and gauge fields
   carry charge: compare to the lowest admissible complete-theory final state,
   not automatically to the reduced scalar threshold m_infinity |Q|.

Negative results are valid outcomes. No new symmetry, deleted interaction or
new field may be silently imposed to obtain a positive answer. A finite
residual symmetry is not the continuous fixed-Q constraint used in the soliton.
An approximate symmetry needs a leakage/lifetime calculation with specified
couplings before a physical persistence claim.
