# Finite one-loop light-doublet matching protocol

2026-09-24. Previous goal turn is progress: 178 qualified checks established
radiative lifting and an explicit lower competing configuration. This stage
addresses the still-missing connection to a low-energy light doublet.

Use the full 68-scalar, 21-vector and 16n-Weyl minimal action in a declared stable
full-basis branch. Choose a norm-only Phi portal k, zero holomorphic pair, and a
positive gap for every physical Delta mode. Select one four-real-component Phi
doublet using a real determinant mass. Its tree mass is zero, the second doublet
has mass squared Mchi2>0, and Phi=h I2/2 is a canonically normalized neutral light
background. Tree radial elimination gives r(h)^2=d^2-k h^2/(4 lambda7), with
lambda_tree=lambda0-k^2/(4 lambda7)>0. These are conditional inputs, not an ACS
selection of the vacuum, scale, or observed flavor structure.

Retain scalar, vector and fermion loops, including mixed heavy/light effects.
Derive heavy eigenvalue jets x(h)=M2+a h^2+b h^4. Compare complete closed spectra
with independently differentiated component Hessians, gauge Grams and Weyl mass
matrices. First compute diagonal real flavor cases with nondegenerate Majorana
and Dirac values; do not advertise them as general noncommuting flavor matching.

Subtract the low-energy light-field potential. Check that its h^4 log(h^2)
coefficient cancels and that the remaining constant, mass and quartic coefficients
agree with high-precision shrinking-field limits. Include the finite kinetic
threshold in the canonical quartic, rather than identifying potential matching
alone with canonically normalized matching. Derive scalar and heavy-vector
kinetic terms using regulated two-point integrals; retain D=4-2 epsilon tensor
contractions. Cross-check the Majorana term with primary seesaw matching.

Use Landau gauge and MS-bar throughout. Check cancellation of matching-scale
dependence against the archived full beta functions and the low-energy one-loop
beta function, for both mass and quartic. Test nonzero portal, vanishing portal,
gauge-only and Yukawa cases and multiple flavor thresholds. A failed check must
be retained and diagnosed before promotion.

Do not infer a measured Higgs mass, select a matching scale, claim a complete
dimension-six EFT, or claim arbitrary-boundary matching from this conditional
calculation. Record the remaining independent inputs and whether tree mass tuning
survives the finite mass threshold.
